02 / Featured capstone

Project Yggdrasil

Yggdrasil is an independent artificial-life research platform. Its long-term goal is to investigate how autonomous artificial individuals, called Gahallians, could develop functional cognitive capabilities and eventually form their own societies. The first proposed experiment is deliberately smaller: can an individual discover an unfamiliar material rule and reproduce it later?
